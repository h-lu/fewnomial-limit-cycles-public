Dear Editors,

Please consider my manuscript, “A superquadratic lower bound for limit cycles of planar polynomial vector fields with N monomials and degree at most N − 1,” as a Research Paper for Nonlinearity.

In their 2024 Nonlinearity article, Gasull and Santana proved a quadratic lower bound when the monomial count is prescribed and the degree is unrestricted. I prove a superquadratic lower bound under a linear degree cap. For every integer N ≥ 48, the construction gives a single polynomial vector field with rational coefficients and coprime components, exactly N monomials counted separately across the two components, and degree at most N − 1. The field has at least F(N) coexisting hyperbolic limit cycles, where F(N) is an explicit positive integer satisfying F(N) ≥ N² log₂ N − 50N².

The mechanism may be of broader interest to readers working on planar dynamics and return maps. When two families of periodic ovals in a separable Hamiltonian merge at a saddle, their regular area-integral coefficients add to give those of the outer family. Quadratic folds and a shared polynomial perturbation use this relation to realize simple return zeros on multiple annuli without exceeding either the monomial or degree budget.

The [Zenodo preprint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24575586) and [tagged reproducibility materials](https://github.com/h-lu/fewnomial-limit-cycles-public/tree/v1.0.1) are publicly available.
