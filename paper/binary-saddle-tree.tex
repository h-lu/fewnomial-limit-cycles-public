\begin{abstract}
A binary hierarchy of horizontal wells allows one perturbation to prescribe regular integral coefficients at successive saddle mergers. A finite minor condition supplies extra crossings between adjacent levels. We give the construction and its specialization to \(261\) terms, degree \(255\) and \(59{,}240\) hyperbolic cycles. The companion archive provides the five interval determinants for this specialization \cite{LuReplay}.
\end{abstract}
